ADOBE ILLUSTRATOR PRACTICAL ASSIGNMENT
=====================================
Category: 01 - Advertising Billboard
Example: 01 - Automobile Advertisement (VELOCITY MOTORS)

1. ASSIGNMENT TITLE
Luxury Automobile Outdoor Billboard Design - "DRIVE YOUR DREAM."

2. ASSIGNMENT OBJECTIVE
Design a high-impact, wide-format outdoor billboard advertisement for a luxury automotive brand ("VELOCITY MOTORS") in Adobe Illustrator using CMYK print setup, layered vector shapes, metallic gold gradients, crisp typographic hierarchy, and embedded studio photography.

3. DESIGN DESCRIPTION
- Brand: VELOCITY MOTORS (custom winged-V metallic gold emblem + geometric wordmark)
- Headline: DRIVE YOUR DREAM. (stacked bold display typography in white & gold)
- Tagline: ENGINEERED FOR PURE PERFORMANCE
- Website: WWW.VELOCITYMOTORS.COM (pill CTA badge)
- Visual Theme: Matte obsidian/charcoal studio backdrop with warm radial lighting, metallic gold border framing, aerodynamic speed-line curves, and a sleek dark metallic luxury sports coupe on the right two-thirds.

4. REQUIRED ADOBE ILLUSTRATOR TOOLS
- Selection Tool (V) & Direct Selection Tool (A)
- Pen Tool (P) for aerodynamic speed lines & custom winged-V logo paths
- Rectangle Tool (M) & Rounded Rectangle Tool for frame & CTA pill
- Shape Builder Tool (Shift+M) & Pathfinder for emblem construction
- Type Tool (T) & Character/Paragraph Panels for headline hierarchy and tracking
- Gradient Tool (G) & Gradient Panel for linear/radial metallic gold & dark studio backdrop
- Eyedropper Tool (I) for sampling palette swatches
- Clipping Mask (Ctrl+7) for framing background glow & car shadow inside artboard bleed
- Align Panel (Shift+F7) for left-margin grid alignment
- Transparency Panel (Multiply/Screen blend modes for floor reflection & ambient glow)
- Appearance Panel for layered gold strokes

5. RECOMMENDED ARTBOARD SIZE & PRINT SETUP
- Real-World Billboard Size: 20 ft x 10 ft (16:9 / 2:1 wide format)
- Illustrator Working Scale (1:10): 1920 pt x 1080 pt (26.67 in x 15 in)
- Color Mode: CMYK (for commercial print production)
- Bleed: 18 pt (0.25 in) on all four sides
- Safe Margin: 44 pt inset from trim edge (keep all text inside safe margin)
- Export Resolution: 300 PPI (for large-format print raster exports)

6. COLOUR PALETTE (CMYK / HEX)
- Obsidian Black:   #0B0B0D | C:75 M:68 Y:67 K:90
- Studio Charcoal:  #1E1E22 | C:70 M:62 Y:58 K:75
- Brushed Gold:     #D4AF37 | C:18 M:28 Y:88 K:2
- Champagne Gold:   #F6E2A1 | C:4  M:10 Y:44 K:0
- Pure White:       #FFFFFF | C:0  M:0  Y:0  K:0

7. ASSETS REQUIRED (Provided in ./Assets/)
- car_luxury_coupe_transparent.png (transparent cutout car)
- car_luxury_coupe_studio.jpg (studio car photo)
- velocity_motors_logo.svg (vector brand logo)
- gold_speed_lines_decor.svg (vector speed lines & border frame)
- dark_obsidian_bg.svg (vector background gradient)
- color_palette.svg (brand colour swatches)
- icon_web_gold.svg & icon_speedometer_gold.svg (supporting vector icons)
- Montserrat-Variable.ttf (OFL typeface)

8. STEP-BY-STEP RECREATION INSTRUCTIONS
Step 1: Create a new CMYK document in Illustrator: 1920 x 1080 pt, Bleed 18 pt all sides, Raster Effects 300 ppi.
Step 2: Set up 6 named layers: Background, Glow_&_Speed_Lines, Vehicle_Asset, Brand_Logo, Typography, Frame_&_Bleed_Guides.
Step 3: On Background, draw a 1956 x 1116 pt rectangle (covering bleed) and apply a radial gradient from #3A2E1A (center right) through #19191C to #0B0B0D.
Step 4: On Glow_&_Speed_Lines, use the Pen Tool (P) to draw three smooth S-curve Bezier paths across the upper-right area and apply a linear gold gradient stroke (#F6E2A1 to #9A7320) with varying stroke weights (4 pt, 2 pt, 1.5 pt).
Step 5: On Vehicle_Asset, File > Place `car_luxury_coupe_transparent.png` on the right two-thirds, scale proportionally, and click Embed in the Control bar. Add a dark soft elliptical shadow beneath the wheels using a radial gradient with Multiply blend mode.
Step 6: On Brand_Logo, construct or place `velocity_motors_logo.svg` at top-left (x=100, y=100), keeping all wing polygon paths editable.
Step 7: On Typography, use the Type Tool (T) to create separate editable text objects:
        - "DRIVE" and "YOUR" in bold geometric sans (#FFFFFF, 118 pt, tight leading)
        - "DREAM." in bold geometric sans with brushed gold fill (#D4AF37, 118 pt)
        - "ENGINEERED FOR PURE PERFORMANCE" in semi-bold tracked uppercase (24 pt, tracking +180, #E5C878)
        - Draw a rounded pill rectangle (#D4AF37 fill) at bottom-left and center "WWW.VELOCITYMOTORS.COM" inside it in #0B0B0D bold text.
Step 8: On Frame_&_Bleed_Guides, add a 2.5 pt gold inner border (inset 44 pt) and diagonal gold corner lines clipped to the artboard.
Step 9: Verify spelling, layer organization, and safe margins. Save as `.ai`, export print-ready `.pdf`, scalable `.svg`, and high-res `.png`/`.jpg`.

9. EXPECTED FINAL OUTPUT
A fully layered, editable Adobe Illustrator billboard artwork with crisp live typography, metallic gold vector accents, and embedded luxury vehicle imagery matching Reference_Image/Example_01_Velocity_Motors_Reference_8K.png.
