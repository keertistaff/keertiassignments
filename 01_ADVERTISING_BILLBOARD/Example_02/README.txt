ADOBE ILLUSTRATOR PRACTICAL ASSIGNMENT
=====================================
Category: 01 - Advertising Billboard
Example: 02 - Real Estate Advertisement (SKYLINE DEVELOPERS)

1. ASSIGNMENT TITLE
Luxury Real Estate Diagonal Split Billboard - "YOUR DREAM HOME AWAITS."

2. ASSIGNMENT OBJECTIVE
Create a bright, high-trust real estate outdoor billboard in Adobe Illustrator using diagonal architectural clipping masks, multi-stop metallic gold dividers, royal navy & white colour blocking, embedded architectural photography, and structured contact iconography.

3. DESIGN DESCRIPTION
- Brand: SKYLINE DEVELOPERS (geometric 3-tower emblem + clean architectural wordmark)
- Headline: YOUR DREAM HOME AWAITS. (split across Royal Navy and Brushed Gold)
- Subheadline: LUXURY 3 & 4 BHK SKY RESIDENCES
- Contact Details: +91 98200 45678 | WWW.SKYLINEDEVELOPERS.COM | MARINE DRIVE, MUMBAI
- Layout Contrast vs Example 01: Uses an angled architectural split composition with daylight glass skyscraper photography on the left and a crisp white/navy typographic column on the right.

4. REQUIRED ADOBE ILLUSTRATOR TOOLS
- Selection Tool (V) & Direct Selection Tool (A)
- Pen Tool (P) for diagonal clipping polygon & gold divider ribbon
- Rectangle Tool (M) for architectural logo towers & bottom contact bar
- Clipping Mask (Ctrl+7) for cropping the skyscraper & sky blend to the diagonal left half
- Type Tool (T) & Character Panel for multi-color headline hierarchy
- Gradient Tool (G) for linear gold bars and subtle sky fade
- Align Panel (Shift+F7) for aligning contact icons and text rows
- Transparency Panel (Screen/Soft Light) for blending the azure sky overlay
- Eyedropper Tool (I) for sampling CMYK swatches

5. RECOMMENDED ARTBOARD SIZE & PRINT SETUP
- Real-World Billboard Size: 20 ft x 10 ft (16:9 wide format)
- Illustrator Working Scale (1:10): 1920 pt x 1080 pt
- Color Mode: CMYK
- Bleed: 18 pt (0.25 in) all sides
- Safe Margin: 48 pt inset

6. COLOUR PALETTE (CMYK / HEX)
- Royal Navy:     #0D2B52 | C:98 M:78 Y:28 K:38
- Azure Accent:   #1C5D99 | C:88 M:52 Y:8  K:0
- Pure White:     #FFFFFF | C:0  M:0  Y:0  K:0
- Brushed Gold:   #C59B27 | C:20 M:36 Y:92 K:6
- Champagne Gold: #F0D78C | C:6  M:14 Y:52 K:0

7. ASSETS REQUIRED (Provided in ./Assets/)
- modern_glass_building_cc0.jpg
- azure_sky_clouds_bg.jpg
- skyline_developers_logo.svg
- icon_phone_gold.svg, icon_web_gold.svg, icon_location_gold.svg, icon_building_gold.svg
- color_palette.svg
- Poppins-Bold.ttf, Poppins-Regular.ttf

8. STEP-BY-STEP RECREATION INSTRUCTIONS
Step 1: Create a 1920 x 1080 pt CMYK document with 18 pt bleed.
Step 2: Create 6 layers: Background_Panel, Clipped_Architecture, Diagonal_Gold_Dividers, Brand_Logo, Typography, Contact_Footer_&_Icons.
Step 3: On Background_Panel, fill the right side with crisp #FFFFFF and add a #0D2B52 bottom contact bar (height 175 pt).
Step 4: On Clipped_Architecture, place `modern_glass_building_cc0.jpg` on the left, overlay `azure_sky_clouds_bg.jpg` with Soft Light / 30% opacity at the top, and clip both inside a diagonal polygon (`[0,0] -> [1120,0] -> [890,1080] -> [0,1080]`) using Ctrl+7.
Step 5: On Diagonal_Gold_Dividers, draw a 22 pt wide slanted metallic gold ribbon along the diagonal cut edge, plus a thin navy accent stripe beside it.
Step 6: On Brand_Logo, construct or place the 3-tower emblem and "SKYLINE DEVELOPERS" wordmark at top-right.
Step 7: On Typography, set "YOUR DREAM" (#0D2B52) and "HOME AWAITS." (#C59B27) in bold sans-serif (86 pt), followed by a gold hairline, "LUXURY 3 & 4 BHK SKY RESIDENCES", and two clean feature callouts.
Step 8: On Contact_Footer_&_Icons, place `icon_phone_gold.svg`, `icon_web_gold.svg`, and `icon_location_gold.svg` aligned cleanly with the contact text in white over the #0D2B52 footer.
Step 9: Save .AI, export 8K PNG reference, print-ready PDF, SVG, and JPG preview.

9. EXPECTED FINAL OUTPUT
A crisp architectural real-estate billboard matching Reference_Image/Example_02_Skyline_Developers_Reference_8K.png with editable layers, clipping masks, and live text.
