ADOBE ILLUSTRATOR PRACTICAL ASSIGNMENT
=====================================
Category: 01 - Advertising Billboard
Example: 03 - Smartphone Advertisement (NOVA MOBILE)

1. ASSIGNMENT TITLE
Flagship Smartphone Launch Billboard - "EXPERIENCE THE FUTURE."

2. ASSIGNMENT OBJECTIVE
Build a futuristic centered-hero product billboard in Adobe Illustrator featuring pure vector product rendering (smartphone chassis, OLED screen gradients, glass reflection), electric-blue radial glow effects, geometric tech grid backgrounds, and icon-driven specification cards.

3. DESIGN DESCRIPTION
- Brand: NOVA MOBILE (4-point star-orbit vector emblem)
- Headline: EXPERIENCE THE FUTURE. (left-column bold futuristic display text)
- Central Hero: Dual-angle flagship vector smartphone (front OLED + rear triple-camera glass module) centered over electric-blue concentric orbital rings.
- Feature Cards (Right Column):
  1. 200MP PRO OPTICS (AI Night & 8K Cinema)
  2. 6000mAh HYPER CHARGE (120W 15-Min Full Boost)
  3. 6.9" 144Hz AMOLED (3000-Nit HDR10+ Display)
- Layout Contrast vs Examples 01 & 02: Centered 3-column symmetrical product showcase with tech-grid vector background and glowing specification cards.

4. REQUIRED ADOBE ILLUSTRATOR TOOLS
- Rounded Rectangle Tool for smartphone body, bezel, OLED display & feature cards
- Gradient Tool (G) & Gradient Panel for titanium frame, electric cyan OLED aura, and glass sheen
- Pen Tool (P) for glowing screen wave curves and diagonal glass reflections
- Polygon/Star Tool for NOVA star emblem and camera lens aperture rings
- Transparency Panel (Screen / Soft Light) for radial neon aura
- Align Panel (Shift+F7) for centered phone stack and evenly spaced right feature cards
- Type Tool (T) for bold left headline and technical spec callouts

5. RECOMMENDED ARTBOARD SIZE & PRINT SETUP
- Real-World Billboard Size: 20 ft x 10 ft (16:9 wide format)
- Illustrator Working Scale (1:10): 1920 pt x 1080 pt
- Color Mode: CMYK (with vibrant electric cyan/blue conversion; RGB optional for DOOH LED boards)
- Bleed: 18 pt all sides

6. COLOUR PALETTE (CMYK / HEX)
- Deep Space Black: #03060D | C:85 M:75 Y:60 K:92
- Midnight Cobalt:  #091B3A | C:96 M:82 Y:32 K:50
- Electric Blue:    #0066FF | C:88 M:58 Y:0  K:0
- Neon Cyan:        #00D4FF | C:70 M:0  Y:5  K:0
- Pure White:       #FFFFFF | C:0  M:0  Y:0  K:0

7. ASSETS REQUIRED (Provided in ./Assets/)
- smartphone_vector_asset.svg
- nova_mobile_logo.svg
- tech_grid_bg.svg
- icon_camera_blue.svg, icon_battery_blue.svg, icon_display_blue.svg, icon_chip_blue.svg
- color_palette.svg
- Orbitron-Bold.ttf

8. STEP-BY-STEP RECREATION INSTRUCTIONS
Step 1: Create a 1920 x 1080 pt document with 18 pt bleed and 6 named layers: Background_Aura, Tech_Grid_&_Rings, Smartphone_Hero, Feature_Cards, Brand_&_Headline, Bleed_Guides.
Step 2: On Background_Aura, draw a full-bleed radial gradient from #0044BB in the center through #07152E to #03060D.
Step 3: On Tech_Grid_&_Rings, draw subtle horizontal/vertical grid lines (10% opacity #00D4FF) and two large concentric orbital circles centered behind the phone.
Step 4: On Smartphone_Hero, construct the flagship phone at center (x=770..1130):
        - Rear camera-angle shadow phone tilted slightly behind to show the triple-lens camera module
        - Front primary phone with rounded titanium chassis, dark bezel, multi-stop electric-blue OLED screen, smooth wave curves, dynamic island pill, and diagonal white glass reflection polygon at 8% opacity.
Step 5: On Brand_&_Headline (Left Column), place the NOVA MOBILE star-orbit logo, bold stacked "EXPERIENCE THE FUTURE." headline, launch tagline "NOVA X1 ULTRA  |  5G AI FLAGSHIP", and glowing cyan CTA pill "WWW.NOVAMOBILE.COM".
Step 6: On Feature_Cards (Right Column), build 3 rounded dark-glass cards with thin #00D4FF strokes, import `icon_camera_blue.svg`, `icon_battery_blue.svg`, and `icon_display_blue.svg`, and add crisp spec titles & subtitles.
Step 7: Save .AI and export 8K PNG reference, PDF, SVG, PNG, and JPG.

9. EXPECTED FINAL OUTPUT
A razor-sharp, 100% vector flagship smartphone billboard matching Reference_Image/Example_03_Nova_Mobile_Reference_8K.png.
