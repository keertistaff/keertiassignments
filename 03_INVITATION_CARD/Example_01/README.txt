ADOBE ILLUSTRATOR PRACTICAL ASSIGNMENT
=====================================
Category: 03 - Invitation Card
Example: 01 - Luxury Wedding Invitation

1. ASSIGNMENT TITLE
Royal Wedding Invitation Card - "Alexander & Victoria".

2. OBJECTIVE
Design an ultra-luxurious, gold-embossed 5x7 inch wedding invitation card in Adobe Illustrator using intricate vector filigree borders, intertwining monogram crests, and serif typography.

3. SPECIFICATIONS
- Artboard Dimensions: 5 x 7 inches (360 x 504 pt)
- Bleed: 9 pt (0.125 in)
- Color Mode: CMYK (Rich Black & Metallic Foil Gradient)
- Palette: Ivory Linen (#FCFBF7), Champagne Gold (#D4AF37), Deep Antique Bronze (#8B6B23), Charcoal (#2C2825)
