ADOBE ILLUSTRATOR PRACTICAL ASSIGNMENT
=====================================
Category: 02 - Infographic Design
Example: 02 - Artificial Intelligence in Daily Life

1. ASSIGNMENT TITLE
Global AI Applications & Ecosystem Infographic.

2. ASSIGNMENT OBJECTIVE
Design a futuristic, high-contrast technology data visualization infographic highlighting 4 major pillars of artificial intelligence (Education, Healthcare, Business Ops, Finance) in Adobe Illustrator.

3. ARTBOARD & SETUP
- Size: A4 Portrait (595.28 x 841.89 pt)
- Color Mode: CMYK / RGB
- Core Pillars:
  1. Smart Healthcare (Predictive Diagnostics & Genomic Research)
  2. Future Education (Hyper-Personalized AI Tutoring)
  3. Enterprise Business (Automated Supply Chains & Intelligent Agents)
  4. Autonomous Finance (Real-Time Fraud Prevention & Algorithmic Trading)

4. ILLUSTRATOR TECHNIQUES
- Glowing vector neural network background
- Circular metric gauges & percentage rings
- Card layout using rounded corner rectangles
- Modern typography pairing (Orbitron & Poppins/Inter)
