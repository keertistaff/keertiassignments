ADOBE ILLUSTRATOR PRACTICAL ASSIGNMENT
=====================================
Category: 02 - Infographic Design
Example: 01 - Digital Marketing Process (6-Step Lifecycle)

1. ASSIGNMENT TITLE
Professional Digital Marketing Process Infographic - 6-Phase Strategic Funnel.

2. ASSIGNMENT OBJECTIVE
Design an agency-grade, vector-based process infographic in Adobe Illustrator showcasing the 6 vital stages of modern digital marketing using clean geometric nodes, flow lines, KPI metrics, and cohesive color psychology.

3. DESIGN DESCRIPTION
- Artboard Size: A4 Portrait (595.28 x 841.89 pt) / 210 x 297 mm
- Color Mode: CMYK (print publication) & RGB (slide deck distribution)
- 6 Key Process Stages:
  1. Market & Audience Research (Deep intent analysis & persona mapping)
  2. Content & Creative Strategy (Multi-channel story architecture)
  3. SEO & Organic Visibility (High-intent keyword discovery & indexing)
  4. Omnichannel Paid Campaigns (Hyper-targeted PPC, social & display)
  5. Conversion Optimization (Landing page UI/UX & frictionless funnels)
  6. Performance Analytics (ROI attribution & automated scale loops)

4. REQUIRED ILLUSTRATOR TOOLS
- Pen Tool (P) for smooth curved connecting flow arrows
- Ellipse & Shape Builder Tools (Shift+M) for circular milestone badges
- Type Tool (T) for headline hierarchy and body copy alignment
- Align & Distribute Panel (Shift+F7) for equidistant stage spacing
- Gradient Tool (G) for vibrant linear stage accents

5. COLOR PALETTE
- Deep Slate: #0F172A
- Royal Blue (Step 1): #3B82F6
- Emerald Green (Step 2): #10B981
- Amber Gold (Step 3): #F59E0B
- Vivid Violet (Step 4): #8B5CF6
- Bright Pink (Step 5): #EC4899
- Cyan Teal (Step 6): #06B6D4

6. STEP-BY-STEP RECREATION
Step 1: Set up A4 document with 9 pt bleed and 6 named layers: Background, Flow_Connectors, Node_Cards, Icons_&_Numbers, Typography, Header_Footer.
Step 2: On Background, draw a 595 x 842 pt rectangle with a deep slate linear gradient (#0B0F19 to #1E293B).
Step 3: On Header_Footer, place headline "DIGITAL MARKETING LIFECYCLE" and subhead "6-Step Strategic Growth Architecture".
Step 4: Draw a winding S-curve connector ribbon down the canvas with dashed highlight lines.
Step 5: Place 6 circular process nodes along the path, alternating left and right.
Step 6: Insert step numbers (01-06), step titles, descriptive bullet points, and vector icons.
Step 7: Add a bottom KPI metrics banner showing "+340% Traffic", "4.8x ROI", "68% CVR Boost".
Step 8: Export SVG, PDF, PNG, and save genuine editable .AI file.
